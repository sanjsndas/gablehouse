About Gable House Theory

An independent editorial journal exploring interior design, architecture, materials, and the art of living well.

We believe that good design is not about decoration — it is about how space, light, and material come together to shape the way we live.

Gable House Theory was founded in 2022 as a counterpoint to the fast-paced, trend-driven world of design media. Our aim is simple: to publish thoughtful, long-form journalism that treats interior design and architecture as cultural practices worthy of serious attention.

We are independent, reader-supported, and editorially driven. Every article is written by our small team of contributing editors and industry experts, and every recommendation is made without paid influence. We take pride in the depth of our research and the clarity of our writing.

“The best interiors are not the ones that shout the loudest, but the ones that quietly support the people who live in them.” — Elena Marsh, Editor-in-Chief

What We Cover

Our editorial scope spans residential and commercial interiors, architectural theory and practice, material innovation, lighting design, furniture, and practical guides for homeowners and design professionals. We are particularly interested in the intersection of sustainability and craft — how we can build and furnish our homes with greater care for the environment and for future generations.

Editorial Team

Elena Marsh

Editor-in-Chief · Copenhagen

Elena is an architecture and interiors writer with over a decade of experience. She has contributed to Dwell, Dezeen, and Architectural Digest, and is the author of two books on Nordic design. She holds a Master's in Architecture from the Royal Danish Academy.

At Gable House Theory, Elena oversees editorial strategy, commissions features, and writes on topics ranging from material innovation to the psychology of space.

James Liu

Senior Editor · London

James is a former practising architect who now writes full-time on architecture and urbanism. He is particularly interested in adaptive reuse, public space, and the social impact of design. He joined Gable House Theory in 2023.

James edits our architecture and sustainable design sections and has a keen eye for the technical details that make buildings work.

We also work with a network of freelance contributors across Europe, North America, and Asia. If you have a story to pitch, please visit our contact page.
