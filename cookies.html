Cookies Policy

How we use cookies and similar technologies on Gable House Theory.
Last updated: 1 March 2025

Gable House Theory ("we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience, analyse site traffic, and understand where our readers come from. This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, which ones we use, and how you can manage them.

Summary: We use a minimal set of cookies — mostly for anonymous analytics and remembering your preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser at any time, though some parts of the site may not work as intended.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the site owners. Cookies do not typically contain any information that personally identifies you, but personal information that we store about you may be linked to the information stored in and obtained from cookies.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies: These are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, and accessibility. You cannot opt out of these without affecting site performance.
  • Analytics cookies: We use these to collect anonymous information about how visitors use our site — such as which pages are visited most often and if users encounter error messages. This helps us improve the journal’s content and navigation.
  • Preference cookies: These remember your settings and choices (e.g., language or region) to provide a more personalised experience.
  • Marketing cookies (rare): On occasion, we may embed content from third parties (e.g., Instagram or Pinterest) that sets cookies. We do not control these third-party cookies — please check the respective platforms’ policies.

3. Specific cookies we set

Below is a list of the main cookies we use and their purpose. We keep our cookie usage minimal and privacy-focused.

Cookie name Type Purpose Duration
gh_session Essential Preserves your session state while navigating the site. Session
gh_prefs Preference Stores your display preferences (e.g., font size). 1 year
_ga Analytics Used by Google Analytics to distinguish unique users (anonymous). 2 years
_gid Analytics Used by Google Analytics to distinguish users (anonymous). 24 hours
cookie_consent Essential Records whether you have accepted the cookie notice. 1 year

Note: This list is illustrative. We do not use any advertising or tracking cookies beyond the anonymous analytics mentioned above.

4. Third-party cookies

Some pages may include embedded content from third parties (for example, a Pinterest save button or an embedded Instagram post). These services may set their own cookies, which we do not control. We recommend reviewing the privacy and cookie policies of these platforms if you have concerns.

5. Your cookie choices

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to refuse or accept cookies, delete existing cookies, and set preferences for specific sites. Check your browser’s help section for instructions.
  • Opt-out tools: For Google Analytics, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Our consent notice: When you first visit our site, you’ll see a brief notice about cookie usage. By continuing to browse, you consent to our use of cookies as described here. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing cookies in your browser.

Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of this and many other websites you visit.

6. Changes to this Cookies Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. The latest version will always be posted on this page, with the “last updated” date at the top. We encourage you to check back periodically.
