Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a design timeless?
Timeless design prioritises proportion, natural materials, and restraint. It avoids trends that date quickly and instead focuses on quality, light, and how a space feels to inhabit over decades.
How do I start planning a renovation?
Begin with a clear brief: how you want to live in the space, your budget, and your must-haves. Then work with an architect or designer to test layouts before committing to finishes.
What is the best lighting for a living room?
Layer your lighting: ambient (ceiling or floor), task (reading), and accent (art or architectural features). Dimmers and warm colour temperatures (2700K–3000K) create flexibility and comfort.
Are sustainable materials more expensive?
Not always. Some, like reclaimed timber or lime plaster, can be cost-competitive. Others may carry a premium, but their durability and lower environmental impact often justify the investment.
