Privacy Policy

How we handle personal data, editorial correspondence, and your rights.
Last updated: 1 March 2025

Gable House Theory ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can exercise your rights. It applies to our website gablehousetheory.com and all related communications.

Summary: We only collect information you voluntarily provide (e.g., through contact forms or emails). We use it solely to respond to you and improve our journal. We never sell or rent your data. You can request access, correction, or deletion at any time.

1. Information we collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you give us: When you fill out our contact form, subscribe to our newsletter, submit an article pitch, or correspond with us by email, you may provide your name, email address, subject matter, and any other details you choose to share.
  • Automatic technical data: Like most websites, we may collect non-personal data such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited. This is used for analytics, security, and improving the site experience. We do not link this data to identifiable individuals unless required by law.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: We use minimal cookies to remember preferences and gather anonymous usage statistics. For more details, see our Cookies Policy.

2. How we use your information

We process your personal data only for legitimate purposes, including:

  • Responding to your inquiries, submissions, or collaboration requests.
  • Sending you editorial updates or newsletters — only if you have opted in.
  • Improving our content, website functionality, and reader experience.
  • Complying with legal obligations or enforcing our terms.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling.

3. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, our legal basis for collecting and using your personal data depends on the context:

  • Consent: When you subscribe to a newsletter or agree to be contacted.
  • Legitimate interests: To respond to your messages, maintain our journal, and improve our services.
  • Legal compliance: Where we must retain data to meet legal requirements.

4. Data sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Trusted partners who help us operate the website (e.g., hosting, analytics) under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal obligations: If required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of Gable House Theory, our readers, or the public.

5. Data retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy. For correspondence, we typically keep emails and contact form submissions for up to 3 years, unless a longer period is required by law. You may request deletion at any time.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data (the "right to be forgotten").
  • Restriction: Limit how we process your data.
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@gablehousetheory.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. International transfers

Our website is operated from Denmark, and our service providers may be located in other countries. When we transfer data outside the EEA, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS) and restricted access to personal data. However, no internet transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children’s privacy

Our journal is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page, with the "last updated" date at the top. We encourage you to review it periodically.

11. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please reach out to:

Gable House Theory
Bredgade 68, 2nd floor
1260 Copenhagen K, Denmark
Email: privacy@gablehousetheory.com

This policy is provided for general information and does not constitute legal advice. For specific concerns, please consult a qualified professional.
